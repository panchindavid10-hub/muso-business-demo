Ground support equipment, or GSE, supports aircraft service activities while an aircraft is on the ground.

Muso Business handles enquiries for equipment supply, spare parts and maintenance. We start with the equipment type, identification and operating requirements you provide.

  • Ground starting equipment and spare parts.
  • Stairs for aircraft support activities.
  • Tyres and other spare parts, subject to reference and application.

What information do I need for a GSE quotation?

Include the equipment type, brand and model if known, required specifications, quantity and destination. For spare parts or maintenance, add the equipment identification and a description of the requirement.

Does an enquiry confirm availability or delivery time?

An enquiry allows us to review your requirement. Availability, delivery time, commercial terms and the applicable warranty are confirmed in the proposal.
