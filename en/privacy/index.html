Website information

A clear view of privacy.

How the quotation form works and what happens when you choose to continue the conversation on WhatsApp.

The form on this website

This is a static Muso Business website. The form lets you prepare a message with your name, company if provided, enquiry type, part or service, quantity where applicable, aircraft or equipment, destination, priority and additional details.

The message is generated in your browser. This website does not have a quotation submission server or an enquiry database. Completing the form or preparing the text does not send an enquiry to Muso Business.

You choose when to share information

When you select “Open WhatsApp”, the prepared message is included in the link that opens that service. At that point, WhatsApp receives the data needed to open the conversation with the text. You must press send within WhatsApp to address the message to Muso Business.

If you select “Copy message”, the text is copied to your device clipboard, subject to your browser permissions. You can review it before pasting and sending it through your chosen channel.

Direct WhatsApp links also open an external service. Its operation and handling of information depend on WhatsApp's terms and policies. This website does not define that service's retention periods or practices.

Information for a commercial enquiry

Share only the details needed to identify your requirement and prepare commercial support. Avoid including passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information in the additional details field.

The information you choose to send through WhatsApp allows the Muso Business team to review your requirement, request clarification and coordinate a quotation or commercial assistance.

Cookies and storage

This version of the website does not include analytics cookies, tracking tools or local storage of form data. You do not need to create a user account.

As part of the connection needed to display the website, the hosting service may process technical data, such as the IP address and browser request information. Its handling depends on the hosting provider. WhatsApp use is governed by its own service terms.

Questions about your information

For questions about information you have shared with Muso Business, contact the same commercial channel: WhatsApp +593 99 275 9073. Explain your enquiry without including unnecessary sensitive information.
