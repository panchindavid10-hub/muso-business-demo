Precision from the first contact

Details make a difference.

A complete enquiry helps identify the item and prepare a proposal that addresses your requirement.

  • Part number (P/N) or a clear description of the item.
  • Quantity, required condition and serial number, if applicable.
  • Helicopter, fixed-wing aircraft or engine model and required documentation.
  • Destination and priority: normal or AOG.
Can I enquire without a part number?

Yes. Describe the item and the aircraft or equipment it is intended for. Identification and compatibility must be confirmed before completing a purchase.

How do I enquire about a helicopter component?

For rotor, transmission or other system references, share the P/N, helicopter model, application and required documentation. Compatibility, condition and availability are confirmed for each enquiry.

How are pricing, delivery and warranty defined?

These are set out in the quotation according to the product and requirement. Include any required conditions or documentation in your initial enquiry.
