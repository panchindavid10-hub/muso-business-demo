What should I include in a maintenance request?

Include the aircraft or equipment identification, location, description of the requirement and priority. Add any relevant technical background. The commercial team may ask for additional information during the review.

Can I indicate an AOG requirement?

Yes. AOG means aircraft on ground. Select this priority and include the location and requirement. Support and timeframes must be confirmed for each case.

How is a service confirmed?

Before coordinating the work, the technical scope, required documentation, timeframes and commercial terms applicable to the case must be agreed.
